<h2 id="first-answer">A short list preview does not mean saved or shared contents are equally short</h2>

Shared text displayName takes 40 Swift Characters; URLs/filenames use other branches. A done-note row displays Text(transcript) with lineLimit(2). Prefix derivation and line layout are different, and neither proves automatic minutes, redaction or truncated originals. Review actual text/transcript, not screenshots, for handoff scope.

## Different from placeholders, summary provenance and material mapping

Placeholders concern missing values, provenance concerns editorial claims, mapping concerns audio/text. Here **list derivation versus stored/shared material** matters. No user records or actual body export is accessed.

## Synthetic preview review

A synthetic record exceeds 40 Characters; only its start is shown. This proves neither only the prefix was saved nor later private content cannot be shared. Two-line cut points also vary with layout, not a fixed character contract.

Inspect actual fields/versions within existing authorization, without expanding private-source access. No screen/assistive-reading/copy/handoff test ran. A view is not a full archive or privacy guarantee.

## FAQ

<h3 id="faq-1">Does prefix40 mean original text changed?</h3>

Not from the derived displayName alone. Actual storage/handoff need review.

<h3 id="faq-2">Does lineLimit2 save only two lines?</h3>

No. It limits layout rather than truncating transcript there.

<h3 id="faq-3">Are URLs/filenames all cut to forty?</h3>

Do not generalize across kind branches or visual layout.

<h3 id="faq-4">Does a failed-row message prove old text cleared?</h3>

No. State-specific display differs from transcript contents.

<h3 id="faq-5">Is a short preview a summary/redaction?</h3>

No. Prefixes/line limits are not semantic summarization or cleansing.

<h3 id="faq-6">Were full user records read/exported?</h3>

No. Static display/data-path scope only, without actual rendering or sharing acceptance.
