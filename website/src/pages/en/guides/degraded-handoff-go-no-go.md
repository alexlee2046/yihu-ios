<h2 id="first-answer">Check the hospital booking first, then who brings documents and handles each ride; a sent checklist is not an accepted arrangement</h2>

When relatives take turns accompanying an older adult to a follow-up appointment, the gap may be a changed booking, a different hospital site, document custody or an unconfirmed return ride—not a missing medical summary. With the person's consent, verify these arrangements in the hospital-accepted original channels, then share only necessary non-sensitive administrative questions with the actual responsible people. A chat acknowledgement, workbench draft or service receipt does not prove registration, a confirmed ride or the person's authorization. Conditions, test results, medication and clinical handover stay in hospital-required channels. Do not wait for this checklist or a message receipt during sudden illness or an emergency.

## Scope: companion logistics, not clinical handover

This checklist helps family members verify administrative roles when taking turns accompanying someone; it does not replace hospital visit instructions. Accompanying a person does not automatically grant record access, signing rights, document collection rights or decision-making authority. Ask the hospital about the actual procedure and authorization for any representation. Do not put names, identity numbers, booking codes, contacts, medical history, test results or medication into this worksheet or an unapproved device or workbench. Actual documents and medical content remain in the person's consented, hospital-accepted channels.

With the required model, permissions and compatible workbench, Yihu can organize voice text and recording notes and prepare drafts. It is not a booking, medical-record, family-authorization or transport-dispatch system. It does not contact hospitals, book vehicles or assess fitness for a transport mode. Recording separately requires the person's permission and compliance with hospital rules; omit it when not permitted. Local recognition does not mean the destination will not store or sync content. Check the device, location, account and access scope before saving.

## Use Yihu in this order

1. **Obtain consent and check the original arrangement.** Without access rights, let the person or an authorized representative check in the original channel. Do not borrow accounts or export complete bookings or medical files.
2. **Keep only administrative scope.** Separate verified arrangements, open items and who checks next. Preserve unconfirmed roles rather than turning intentions into commitments.
3. **Review key terms word by word.** Compare dates, hospital site, arrival requirements, outbound and return roles, and negation. “Ride not yet confirmed” must not become “confirmed.” Recording order is not event order.
4. **Check the target and earlier attempt.** Explicit insertion binds to the workbench and page present when the note was opened; stop if the target changes. Not every page supports insertion. A visible draft is neither sent nor registered with the hospital.
5. **Obtain feedback for each separate scope.** The actual responsible person confirms in the original authorized channel. A sending receipt is not driver acceptance; checked document categories do not prove the actual documents are complete. Without feedback, retain the open state.
6. **Stop on uncertainty and inspect original records.** The note's single handoff reservation covers direct insertion and staging. Do not clear markers, rename notes or switch targets to blindly resend after failure or uncertainty. New operations do not prove old ones; copying can leave duplicates too.

Direct insertion has a 3,000 Swift Character limit; longer notes use the existing Markdown attachment-staging path. Staging, uploading, sending and human acceptance are different stages. Do not use attachments to bypass refusal. There is no offline outbox, automatic notification, automatic retry, cross-target deduplication or recall of every copy added by this page. Restored connectivity does not prove the booking and ride arrangements still apply.

## Frequently asked questions

<h3 id="faq-booking">Does sending the checklist to family confirm the appointment?</h3>

No. The hospital's original channel establishes the booking; explicit feedback from the relevant people establishes family roles. A successful send replaces neither.

<h3 id="faq-documents">Can I put medical records, identity documents and booking codes in the download?</h3>

No. It checks document categories and administrative custody scope only. Originals and medical content stay in the person's consented, hospital-accepted channels, not this page or an unapproved workbench.

<h3 id="faq-clinical">Should the person stop medication or fast before the follow-up?</h3>

This page provides no such advice. Verify the hospital's and qualified professionals' instructions for the individual. Do not infer requirements from someone else's experience or a voice draft.

<h3 id="faq-consent">Does a companion automatically get signing or medical-record access rights?</h3>

No. Follow the person's wishes, the hospital's actual rules and the relevant authorization process. This worksheet is not authorization or identity-verification evidence.

<h3 id="faq-transport">Will Yihu chase a driver who has not replied or arrange a vehicle?</h3>

This page promises neither. No reply remains unconfirmed; check through the original contact method. Ask the relevant service professionals about assistance or safety rather than having this checklist assess it.

<h3 id="faq-retry">The page reloaded after insertion. Can I hand off the note again?</h3>

Check the original destination first. Reload, a missing receipt or restored connectivity does not prove the original operation did not happen. Do not bypass the single handoff reservation or use new operations to prove old ones. Use existing reliable channels for urgent matters.
