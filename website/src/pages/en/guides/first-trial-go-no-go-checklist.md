<h2 id="first-answer">Start with one useful check</h2>

Check your web workspace, source-install setup, usable speech models and a safe sample draft first. Prepare whatever is missing before involving real business material.

## Try this order

1. Name the task, for example placing one spoken thought into your own web draft.
2. Check Mac/Xcode/XcodeGen, your signing, iOS 18+ and a trusted HTTPS workspace.
3. Confirm compatible models are downloaded and usable, then arrange mic permission and recording consent where needed.
4. Try one synthetic phrase, inspect its wording and destination, then decide whether to continue.

<div role="region" aria-label="Choose your next step" tabindex="0">

| Your situation | What to do | Before continuing |
|---|---|---|
| Task fit | An existing Agent web workspace with less app switching. | For text alone, a lightweight transcription tool may be simpler. |
| Installation | Source-build tools and your own signing. | Follow setup without borrowing accounts or signatures. |
| Models and capture | Usable models, a safe setting and consent. | Then test one phrase. |
| Sample destination | A suitable unsent draft. | Understand that the page may save/sync it. |

</div>

## FAQ

<h3 id="faq-step-1">How long will setup take?</h3>

Allow for your build, model downloads and service setup. This checklist is not a setup or performance promise.

<h3 id="faq-step-2">Can the website preview replace a device trial?</h3>

It explains the interaction; test the actual device, model and destination separately.

<h3 id="faq-step-3">What if I do not have a workspace?</h3>

Check whether transcription alone meets your need. Yihu supplies source client/setup instructions; you provide the backend.

<h3 id="faq-step-4">Can I import this checklist into the app?</h3>

It is a manual checklist, not an app configuration/import format. Continue with the setup guide.
