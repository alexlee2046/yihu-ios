<h2 id="first-answer">Start with one useful check</h2>

Start with the App Store edition, your own HTTPS workspace and account, and one safe sample draft. Download the optional speech model (about 950 MB) when you want voice input.

## Try this order

1. Name the task, for example placing one spoken thought into your own web draft.
2. [Get Yihu on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yihu/id6819057500), then connect your trusted HTTPS workspace. Store installation needs no Mac or self-signing; check the listing for device and regional availability.
3. Confirm compatible models are downloaded and usable, then arrange mic permission and recording consent where needed.
4. Try one synthetic phrase, inspect its wording and destination, then decide whether to continue.

## FAQ

<h3 id="faq-step-1">How long will setup take?</h3>

Allow time for app installation, optional model downloads and your workspace setup. Developers choosing a source build can follow the separate build/signing instructions.

<h3 id="faq-step-2">Can the website preview replace a device trial?</h3>

It explains the interaction; test the actual device, model and destination separately.

<h3 id="faq-step-3">What if I do not have a workspace?</h3>

Check whether transcription alone meets your need. Yihu connects to your workspace; you provide its address, account and backend.

<h3 id="faq-step-4">Can I import this checklist into the app?</h3>

It is a manual checklist, not an app configuration/import format. Continue with the setup guide.
