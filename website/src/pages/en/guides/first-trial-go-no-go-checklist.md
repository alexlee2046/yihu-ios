<h2 id="first-answer">Start with one useful check</h2>

Choose store installation or a separate source build, then check your own workspace, voice-model readiness if needed and a safe synthetic draft. Do not use real business material for the first check.

## Try this order

1. Name the task, for example placing one spoken thought into your own web draft.
2. The verified US store edition 0.1.0 installs without a Mac or self-signing. Source developers separately need Mac/Xcode/XcodeGen and their own signing. Use iOS 18+ and your own trusted HTTPS workspace.
3. If you choose native voice input, prepare the optional speech-model download, microphone permission and recording consent. Viewing the synthetic web preview does not download the Yihu speech model or record audio.
4. Try one synthetic phrase, inspect its wording and destination, then decide whether to continue.

## FAQ

<h3 id="faq-step-1">How long will setup take?</h3>

Allow for your chosen installation path, any speech-model download you elect to use and your own service setup. This checklist promises neither setup time nor performance.

<h3 id="faq-step-2">Can the website preview replace a device trial?</h3>

It explains the interaction; test the actual device, model and destination separately.

<h3 id="faq-step-3">What if I do not have a workspace?</h3>

Check whether transcription alone meets your need. Yihu has a verified US store edition and public source, but provides no hosted workspace or backend account.

<h3 id="faq-step-4">Can I import this checklist into the app?</h3>

It is a manual checklist, not an app configuration/import format. Continue with the setup guide.
