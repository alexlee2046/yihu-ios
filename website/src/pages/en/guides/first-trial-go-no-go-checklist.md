<h2 id="first-answer">Five minutes is a decision timebox, not a setup or performance promise</h2>

Yihu is currently a source release requiring your own signing configuration, compatible models and HTTPS workbench. Before building or trying real device paths, use this card to distinguish available, unknown and missing prerequisites and who confirms them. It records no audio, writes no task and provisions no service. You may stop with unresolved items; actual setup remains in the connection guide, and quick evaluation is not installation/device acceptance.

## Decide first rather than duplicating setup instructions

If you need an immediately available group voice channel, do not evaluate this browser shell as that product. Without your own Agent workbench, a website demo does not establish a supplied backend. Continue these checks when the intended outcome is reviewable text in your own draft. Web access, model availability, microphone permission and service-write authority need separate records.

## Four decisions, not a misleading completion score

- **Ready to enter the existing setup flow**: evidence and ownership are clear for an authorized synthetic-draft trial. This is not feature acceptance.
- **Waiting for confirmation**: signing or service arrangements may need the maintainer. Record the role and question, not an inferred answer.
- **Missing prerequisite**: stop the affected trial without a workbench/model; do not create paid services, accounts or public ports here.
- **Wrong product fit**: standalone Watch approval or group field intercom needs are not satisfied by opening a web page.

A missing authorized destination cannot be offset by several other checked boxes. Do not score the website demo as evidence from your own device.

This is a manual decision record, not an app configuration/import format. It changes no settings. Do not put Team identifiers, personal accounts, service tokens or real audio into public examples.

## FAQ

<h3 id="faq-five-minutes">Is installation and a first draft guaranteed in five minutes?</h3>

No. Five minutes is only a short evaluation timebox that can end with unknowns. Builds, signing, downloads and service readiness may require separate preparation.

<h3 id="faq-demo">Does the website demo establish my device is configured?</h3>

No. It plays synthetic material without accessing your microphone/workbench. Actual app version, signing, permissions and destination compatibility need their own checks.

<h3 id="faq-account">Can Yihu provision a workbench account for me?</h3>

No account or hosted service is offered here. Use your existing authorized service process; an example origin is not a working system supplied by Yihu.

<h3 id="faq-permission">Can I disable certificate or verification checks for a quick trial?</h3>

No. A timebox does not bypass TLS, model verification, required speaker models, host refusal or write authority. Stop when prerequisites are absent.

<h3 id="faq-reply">Does a go decision include Radar writes and notifications?</h3>

No. Replies need separate server authority, phone confirmation/readback. Notifications and Watch pairing have independent prerequisites; draft readiness does not accept them.

<h3 id="faq-retest">Where should I resume after missing prerequisites are confirmed?</h3>

Recheck the target and existing content before entering the connection guide. Resolve prior handoff uncertainty through its existing state; a new evaluation does not authorize resending.
