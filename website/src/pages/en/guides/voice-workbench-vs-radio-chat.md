<h2 id="first-answer">Start with one useful check</h2>

To reach a person immediately, start with your team's communication tool. To turn a thought into reviewable text, consider Yihu around the web workspace you already use.

## Try this order

1. Identify the recipient: a colleague, your web workspace or a note for later.
2. Compare the same task against accounts/devices, connectivity, storage and confirmation steps.
3. Try one synthetic task, inspect the result and decide whether setup is worthwhile.

<div role="region" aria-label="Choose your next step" tabindex="0">

| Your situation | What to do | Before continuing |
|---|---|---|
| Reach a person on site | Radio equipment matching the system and local rules. | Check recipients, device instructions and communication procedures. |
| Reach an existing group | Your team's voice-chat service. | Inspect the particular product's account, network and retention settings. |
| Continue an Agent web task | Yihu and a trusted HTTPS workspace. | Prepare models/signing, review the draft and choose when to send. |
| Organize a recording later | A recorder/transcription tool, or Yihu voice notes. | Retain the original and choose how to organize and hand off text. |

</div>

## FAQ

<h3 id="faq-step-1">Is Yihu a group voice channel?</h3>

It focuses on your own workspace and notes. Use an appropriate communication tool for live person-to-person contact.

<h3 id="faq-step-2">Does pairing a headset settle compatibility?</h3>

Test a recording and inspect actual input. Pairing and music output do not establish capture readiness.

<h3 id="faq-step-3">What if I only want a transcript?</h3>

A lighter transcription path may be enough. Consider Yihu when you already use an Agent web workspace.

<h3 id="faq-step-4">How should I compare privacy or performance?</h3>

Inspect each tool's actual settings and data flow, and try your equipment. No category-wide range, latency or accuracy ranking is supplied.
