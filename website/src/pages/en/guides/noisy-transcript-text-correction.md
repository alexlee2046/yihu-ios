<h2 id="first-answer">Start with one useful check</h2>

Play back available source audio and inspect names, numbers and negation. Mark unsupported wording as unconfirmed, then revise it in an editor or draft that is actually available.

## Try this order

1. Keep the original and audio. Check uncertain words rather than inferring facts from fluent phrasing.
2. Revise in an existing, permitted editor and keep open questions visible.
3. Inspect any old destination draft, clipboard text and attachment version separately.
4. Review before sending. If the previous handoff result is uncertain, pause and inspect it first.

| Your situation | What to do | Before continuing |
|---|---|---|
| Retained text / original audio | Keep the review evidence. | Text edits do not change source audio. |

## FAQ

<h3 id="faq-step-1">Will every copy update automatically?</h3>

Inspect destinations and attachments separately; there is no cross-copy updater.

<h3 id="faq-step-2">Can I hand it off again after an edit?</h3>

Open the draft already at the destination first. Review or edit that copy; do not hand it off again while the earlier result is unclear.

<h3 id="faq-step-3">What if a number or name is unclear?</h3>

Leave it unconfirmed and check the source or participant rather than supplying a plausible answer.

<h3 id="faq-step-4">Does corrected text prove noise performance?</h3>

Manual correction and original recognition performance differ. Test the particular device and environment separately.
