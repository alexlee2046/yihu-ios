<h2 id="first-answer">Sufficient evidence is not maximum collection; check retention and disclosure scope first</h2>

Teams exporting existing follow-up tasks to Radar JSON keep the source's permissions. Yihu is not a CRM, customer collector or trade-data archive. This guide checks unnecessary text, identities and URL parameters in material being retained/shared, separating private originals from public synthetic examples. It is not an automatic redactor, compliance certification or delete-all-copies feature.

## Different from ownership, claim audit and verification

Ownership checks responsibility/storage; evidence audit checks completion claims; second checks assess evidence for one anomaly claim. Here **already authorized material is being prepared for retention/disclosure, so minimize content and mark private scope**. It does not assign owners or adjudicate business truth.

| Screenshots/logs | Accounts, feeds, customer text or device identity visible? | Synthetic content and non-sensitive outcomes publicly | Automatic screenshot masking |
| Documents/clipboard/attachments | Expanded copying, syncing or access? | Check actual destination rules and necessary copies | One deletion recalls everything |

## A fully synthetic public explanation

```text
Source: Sample project, not a real customer/order.
Scope: Field shape and visible state, without original business text.
Evidence example: https://example.invalid/evidence/sample
Archive: Confirm existing authorized rules; no new share destination created.
Unknown: Private-source retention/access remains with the original process.
```

The reserved-domain example supplies no real evidence and should not be placed in a live ledger as an original record. Public synthetic explanations and private authentic evidence serve different purposes. Removing all provenance does not make a public example proof of a real customer item.

## From authorized material to a minimal package

Define retention purpose and authorized readers before deciding whether text, audio, identifiers or a controlled original reference is necessary. Mark unavailable evidence unreviewed rather than demanding more data. Do not provision accounts, public shares or bulk private-task exports under a complete-archive label.

Native Radar retains source text; `safeEvidenceURLs` selecting HTTPS does not strip queries. Text handoff does not delete original audio or other copies. After editing, still inspect note reservation/destination state. A redacted draft cannot bypass one-handoff or uncertain-stop guards.

## FAQ

<h3 id="faq-crm">Does Yihu automatically archive customer correspondence and evidence?</h3>

No CRM capability is asserted. This is minimization guidance for your authorized task data, without customer collection, email or automatic record creation.

<h3 id="faq-redact">Are HTTPS evidence links automatically redacted?</h3>

Do not infer that. Parsing checks shape, not removal of parameters, identity or business paths. Review public material manually under actual permissions.

<h3 id="faq-synthetic">Does replacing a name with role A guarantee anonymity?</h3>

No. Text, time, paths, orders and combinations can remain identifying. No anonymization certification is supplied; use fully synthetic public examples.

<h3 id="faq-export">Does Radar visibility permit exporting all history?</h3>

No. Check original-item, service and team scope. Reading, bulk export and publication are different operations.

<h3 id="faq-delete">Does deleting phone records delete remote copies after archiving?</h3>

No. Workbenches, clipboard, attachments, account sync and backups can retain separate copies. Check actual policies without a remote-recall guarantee.

<h3 id="faq-audit">Does minimization establish acceptance of the real business event?</h3>

Only actual authorized evidence supports a limited conclusion. A synthetic public explanation is not the original, approval, delivery or handling record.
