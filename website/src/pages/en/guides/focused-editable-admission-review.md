<h2 id="first-answer">Focus is one prerequisite, not permission for every field to receive native text</h2>

The guarded adapter considers textarea, specified text-like inputs and contenteditable, then checks connection, disabled/readOnly-with-keyboard-exception, inert/aria and visibility conditions. Known Collie structures must prove a unique draft mode. Visible boxes/placeholders do not establish universal form identification or compatibility.

## Different from origin, bridge and keyboard review

Origin checks boundaries, bridges check branches, keyboards check sessions. Here **DOM candidate eligibility** matters. No clicks, injections or attribute changes ran.

## Synthetic DOM review

A focused password input is not in this text-type set. An ordinary textarea still needs usability/draft conditions. Seeing a box proves no success; do not remove disabled/aria-busy to bypass the UI.

Normal editing remains scoped and unknown frameworks unguaranteed. Main-frame/shadow tracking/isSafeDraft are not security/identity certification or new general form automation.

## FAQ

<h3 id="faq-1">Does focus guarantee insertion?</h3>

No. Type/state/page/adapter checks remain.

<h3 id="faq-2">Are all readonly elements treated identically?</h3>

No. An App keyboard-policy exception exists; do not extend it to bypass other locks.

<h3 id="faq-3">Are every iframe/shadow supported?</h3>

No. Main-frame scope and observable focus targets differ.

<h3 id="faq-4">Does a placeholder identify the task draft?</h3>

No. Structure/state/original-target conditions remain.

<h3 id="faq-5">Do known Collie markers relax checks?</h3>

No. Known structures must prove draft conditions, not exploit generic assumptions.

<h3 id="faq-6">Were page attributes changed?</h3>

No. Fixed source only, not framework compatibility certification.
