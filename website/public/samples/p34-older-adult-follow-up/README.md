# 老人复诊陪诊行政交接空白表 / Older-adult follow-up companion checklist

核对已有预约、资料类别、接送与陪诊分工，不填写个人或医疗正文。
Check the existing appointment, document categories, transport and companion roles without personal or medical content.

- 中文：checklist.zh.json；English: checklist.en.json。十二项逐一对应，翻译不是两次交接。
- Twelve equivalent checks; translations are not separate handovers.
- 空白人工工作表，不是App导入格式、医院登记、预约确认、授权书或一呼原生审批记录。网页和文件不会接收或保存填写内容。
- Blank manual worksheet, not an app import, hospital registration, appointment confirmation, authorization form or native Yihu approval record. The page and files do not receive or save entries.
- null / unfilled 表示未填写，不代表通过、无风险、不适用或已完成。
- null / unfilled means unfilled, not passed, safe, not applicable or complete.
- checked_reference 只放获准的最小非敏感引用；checked_scope 只记已核范围；unresolved 保留疑问。不要填写姓名、证件号、预约码、联系方式、病情、检验结果、用药或私有正文。
- checked_reference is a minimal authorized non-sensitive reference; checked_scope is verified scope; unresolved retains gaps. No names, identity numbers, booking codes, contacts, conditions, test results, medication or private content.
- 本表只核“谁核哪项、哪一步待确认”；实际证件与医疗资料留在本人同意且医院认可的原渠道。陪诊不自动获得代签、看记录或替本人决定的权利。
- Check who verifies each item and what remains open. Actual documents stay in the person's consented, hospital-accepted channel. Accompanying someone does not grant signing, record access or decision-making authority.
- 录音另核本人许可和医院规则；不获准就不用录音。本机识别不保证目标不保存/不同步，另存前核设备、位置与可读范围。
- Recording requires the person's permission and hospital rules; omit it when not permitted. Local recognition does not guarantee destinations will not store or sync content. Check device, location and access before saving.
- 草稿不等预约，发送不等接送人确认；不确定先查原目标，不盲重发，不代他人确认。
- A draft is not a booking; sending is not driver acceptance. Check the original destination on uncertainty; do not blindly resend or acknowledge for others.
- 不提供诊断、用药、禁食、急救、行动能力评估或转运安全建议。相关问题找医院专业人员，紧急情况不等清单或消息回执。
- No diagnosis, medication, fasting, emergency, mobility assessment or transport-safety advice. Ask qualified hospital staff; do not wait for this checklist or a message receipt in an emergency.
